Gweilo · Lion Rock West Face · 獅子山 · 6a+ (HK), 4 pitches, ≈75 m

An HK classic (FA 1991). Starts from the top of Prelude (F4). Pitches: 5 (22 m) · 6a+ corner crack (12 m) · 5+ exposed traverse (13 m) · 6a+ (28 m) to the summit. Walk off on the hiking trail (no abseils). About 6 km from Dorsett (taxi 15–20 min to Lion Rock Park / Wang Tau Hom), approach ≈ 30–40 min. Beacon Hill is right next to it (≈ 1 km).

🚕 Taxi from Dorsett → Lion Rock Park (Hung Mui Kuk Rd) · Lion Rock trail · no ferry

Does it fit the plan? Yes — no ferry constraints; best instead of / alongside Beacon Hill on Mon 26 Oct (no queues on a weekday). Start early (heat; the face is west-facing in the afternoon).

Bolt status (latest info):

  • hongkongclimbing.com (warning “as of October 2020”): some bolts on pitches 3 and 4 chopped, the traverse around the arête unprotected, summit anchor removed — you must build your own (trad / slings around boulders).
  • theCrag: “Bolts chopped” warning dated 3 Oct 2020; the neighbouring Wards Groove entry says that since 2025 pitch 4 needs trad protection + your own anchor at the top.
  • UKC logbook: 16 Nov 2023 — a party bailed at the start of pitch 3 (chopped bolts); 25 Jan 2024 — “last pitch was cut”; 16 Nov 2024 — “Trad climbed it”.
  • No information found about rebolting after 2020 (last rebolt: F. Haden, Jan 2014 — Mountain Project).

Gear (our cautious recommendation — not from the topo): 15 quickdraws + 4–5 long slings, 60 m rope, helmet; a set of medium–large nuts + cams approx. 0.4–2 (BD) / “1.5–2.5 friends” per hkclimbing — in practice 1× each size, optionally 2× #1–#2; 2 × 120 cm slings + HMS lockers for the summit anchor. Without trad gear — pitches 1–2 only, then abseil.
